30 spots across America where you can still push the button and watch the magic happen. Spot one we missed? Submit a sighting!

Who runs these things? Two family companies keep the whole fleet alive: Mold-A-Rama® Inc. of Brookfield, IL services the Midwest / Texas / Tennessee machines, and Unique Souvenirs Inc. of Florida runs the ones branded Mold-A-Matic™, mostly around Florida. Different stickers on the front — same 1960s machines, same hot plastic smell.
